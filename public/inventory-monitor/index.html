Transfers: every move between your locations — Inventory → Bazaar (bazaar add), Bazaar → Inventory (bazaar remove), Inventory → Display (display add), Display → Inventory (display remove), Inventory → Market (item market listing), Market → Inventory (listing removed), and Bazaar → Sold / Market → Sold (a player bought from you).
